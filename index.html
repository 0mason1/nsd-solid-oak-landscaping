What We Do

Full-Service Landscaping for Every Need

From pristine weekly maintenance to dramatic landscape renovations, we bring the skills and equipment to make your outdoor space something to be proud of.

Lawn Mowing & Maintenance

Consistent, reliable mowing schedules tailored to your lawn's needs. We edge, trim, and blow every visit — leaving your yard looking professionally manicured week after week.

🌳

Landscape Design & Installation

Transform your outdoor space with custom landscape designs. We handle everything from plant selection and layout to full installation — built for Charlotte's climate.

Tree & Shrub Trimming

Keep your trees and shrubs healthy and shapely with expert pruning. We remove dead growth, shape overgrown plants, and improve the overall curb appeal of your property.

Mulching & Bed Edging

Spring and fall cleanups to clear debris, prep beds, cut back perennials, and get your yard ready for the season ahead — so you never fall behind on maintenance.

About Solid Oak

Rooted in Charlotte, Built on Craftsmanship

Solid Oak Landscaping was founded right here in Charlotte with one mission: to give every client a yard they're genuinely proud of. We're a locally owned team that treats your property the way we'd treat our own — with care, precision, and honest communication at every step.

Over 14 years, we've built long-term relationships with hundreds of Charlotte homeowners across neighborhoods like Ballantyne, Myers Park, Dilworth, and SouthPark. Our crew shows up on time, works clean, and never cuts corners.
